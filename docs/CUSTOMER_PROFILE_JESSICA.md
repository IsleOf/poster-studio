# Customer Profile: Jessica

Use this profile when making decisions about product copy, mockup direction, listing defaults, image generation prompts, visual polish, and profitable product expansion.

## Target Buyer

- Name archetype: Jessica
- Age/income: 38, high-income
- Buying behavior: habitual Etsy buyer, comfortable paying for personalized gifts and polished home decor
- Taste profile: modern, minimalist luxury; organic-modern interiors; bespoke artwork that looks curated and expensive
- Purchase motivation: emotionally meaningful keepsakes that still fit into a high-end home
- Visual bar: avoid novelty/crafty mockups; favor premium editorial interiors, restrained typography, soft warm light, neutral materials, and framed wall-art presentation

## Mockup Image Prompt Formula

Use this prompt formula for product room mockups:

```text
A photorealistic, high-end interior design mockup of a [vertical/horizontal] [matte black/natural white oak] picture frame. The frame is hanging on a clean, warm-white wall in a bright, airy millennial living room. Below the frame is a minimalist light wood console table decorated with a ceramic vase containing dried pampas grass and a stack of expensive coffee table books. Natural, soft sunlight is streaming in from a large window out of frame, casting beautiful, gentle shadows across the wall. Premium, photorealistic, 8k resolution, editorial interior photography.
```

Recommended variants:

- Use `vertical` for most poster products.
- Use `horizontal` for route maps, multi-map sets, or wide city maps.
- Use `matte black` for monochrome street maps and black/white star maps.
- Use `natural white oak` for colored first-home and heart-shaped map products.
- Keep wall color warm white, not cold grey.
- Avoid clutter, novelty props, saturated decor, visible people, or low-end apartment styling.

## Profitable Product Expansion Ideas

Prioritize formats that increase average order value or target high-income gift occasions.

1. Marathon and running route maps
   - Buyer intent: achievement gift, Boston/New York/London marathon keepsake, runner spouse gift
   - Personalization: runner name, bib number, race date, finish time, highlighted route
   - Pricing logic: higher perceived effort than a static map; good for premium digital and framed options

2. Topographical / mountain elevation maps
   - Buyer intent: hikers, skiers, national park lovers, luxury cabin decor
   - Personalization: mountain name, coordinates, elevation, date climbed/skied
   - Premium locations: Mount Rainier, Grand Tetons, Aspen/Snowmass, Yosemite, Banff

3. `Our Journey` multi-map sets
   - Buyer intent: wedding/anniversary wall decor
   - Structure: three matching maps: `Where We Met`, `Where We Engaged`, `Where We Married`
   - Pricing logic: triples order value and solves a larger wall-decor need

4. Lake house / bathymetric depth maps
   - Buyer intent: second-home decor, parent gifts, lake-house owners
   - Personalization: lake name, family name, established date, cabin coordinates
   - Premium locations: Lake Tahoe, Lake George, Lake Como, Lake Michigan, Lake Minnetonka

5. Venue map wedding suite add-ons
   - Buyer intent: brides buying gifts and wedding stationery
   - Products: ceremony venue map, reception venue map, welcome sign map, guest-book print
   - Pricing logic: bundle with heart map or star map for higher AOV

6. Pet memorial or adoption-location maps
   - Buyer intent: emotionally driven memorial gifts
   - Products: `Where We Found You`, `Forever in Our Hearts`, adoption day star/map print
   - Use restraint: premium, quiet memorial aesthetic, not cartoonish

## Implementation Notes For Agents

- When adding default text suggestions, keep copy design-specific and occasion-specific.
- Do not make all designs share the same generic romantic text list.
- Use high-income gift language: `forever`, `first home`, `where our story started`, `the place that matters`, `under this sky`.
- Avoid cheap-sounding copy: `best boyfriend ever`, `couple goals`, `bae`, overly trendy slang.
- For generated mockups, use interior prompts above and keep the poster itself clean, legible, and centered in a premium frame.
