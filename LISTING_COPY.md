# Listing Copy — The Mapped Moment

Canonical, reusable copy for Etsy listings, shop announcement, and policies.
Swap **[bracketed]** placeholders per listing. Keep this in sync with the shop.

> ⚠️ Etsy removed the free-text *shipping policy* field — shipping copy lives in the
> **listing description** and **Shop Announcement** only. Returns/cancellations are set
> in Policy settings (structured). See CLAUDE.md → "Markets, products & shipping policy".

> **Copy principle (audited):** the description is ordered to lead with the *emotional hook
> and benefits*, then how-it-works, then reassurance. The less-popular terms (made-to-order =
> non-refundable, revision cap, edit window) are **never up top** — they live in the
> **"The details"** / **"Fine print"** block at the very bottom, phrased as warmly as honesty
> allows. They are present and findable (so a buyer who reads the whole listing or our policies
> is informed), but the buyer's first impression is the keepsake, the quality, and free US
> shipping — not the restrictions.

## Listing description block (paste into each physical-print / framed listing)

**✦ [Star Map / Street Map] Poster — personalised & made just for you**
A custom keepsake of your moment — your exact location, date, and names, captured forever as
gallery-quality wall art. The perfect anniversary, wedding, newborn, or "the night we met" gift. 💛

**🖼 Gallery quality, made to order** — printed on 180gsm archival fine-art paper with
fade-resistant giclée inks and carefully packed to arrive flawless. **Framed orders arrive
ready to hang** (no assembly) in your choice of black or white frame.

**⚡ Need it for a date? You're covered** — the moment you order you also get an **instant
print-ready digital file**, so even if the post is slow, your gift is ready today.

**How it works** — about 2 minutes
1️⃣ Tap **[👉 Design your map here]** and personalise it (location, date, names).
2️⃣ At the end you'll get a short **design code** — copy it.
3️⃣ Back on Etsy, paste that code into the **"Personalisation"** box at checkout.
4️⃣ We print & ship your poster — and your digital file is emailed right after. ✨

**🚚 Shipping**
- 🇺🇸 US — **FREE tracked shipping**, ~3–7 days (after 1–3 day production)
- 🇬🇧 UK & 🇪🇺 EU — tracked, ~7–14 days · 🇨🇦 Canada & 🇦🇺 Australia — ~1–3 weeks

**💛 Our promise to you**
- **Damaged or lost in transit?** Send a photo and we'll ship a **free replacement** — no quibbles.
- **Spotted a typo?** Message us within **24 hours** and we'll correct & reprint it free.
- **Digital files** come with **free edits for a full 30 days** — tweak the names, date, or
  styling whenever you like.

> 💡 *Tip: please design via the link first.* Typing details into the Personalisation box
> *without a design code* means we can't generate your map automatically — we'll follow up by
> hand, which can delay your order.

---
**The details** *(for anyone who likes the full picture)*
Each piece is personalised and printed just for you, so we're unable to accept change-of-mind
returns — but if anything arrives damaged or wrong, we'll always make it right (see "Our promise"
above). Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees; for a specific date, order early (the
instant digital file has you covered either way). Need to cancel? Just message us within 24
hours of ordering. Digital edits are included free for 30 days and up to 3 updated versions per
order — beyond that, message us and we'll happily help.

## Policy settings (structured — not free text)

These are the formal, "dig into the ToS" sources. Keep them consistent with the warmer
description copy above; the description leads with benefits, these state the terms plainly.

- **Returns & exchanges:** custom **"No returns or exchanges"** (personalised / made-to-order).
  Faulty / damaged / lost-in-transit items are replaced free (not a "return" — a remake).
- **Cancellations:** accept within **24 hours** (pairs with the paused-Prodigi release window → cancel free).
- **Digital edit policy** (state in the FAQ / listing fine print): free edits for **30 days**
  from delivery, up to **3 updated versions** per order; further changes by request.
- **EU right of withdrawal / T&C:** see `EU_WITHDRAWAL.md` (personalised goods exempt under CRD
  Art.16(c); model form + ODR link).
